San Mateo Rizal
Local Youth Development Office

A youth-driven and volunteer-led institution under the Municipal Government of San Mateo, Rizal. Dedicated to leadership formation, active civic participation, and sustainable youth development across our barangays.

Explore Office Mandate
Organization Profile

Building Good Governance & Civic Engagement

The LYDO develops, coordinates, and evaluates youth initiatives anchored on the Local Youth Development Plan (LYDP).

🏛️

Local Governance

Assists the Local Youth Development Council (LYDC) and Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) federations in crafting youth policies that directly impact community welfare.

🎯

Youth Empowerment

Provides skill-building summits, democratic capacity workshops, and civic education to equip young San Mateños with active public service competencies.

🤝

YORP Accreditation

Assists grassroots community youth organizations with Youth Organization Registration Program (YORP) certification to ensure legal recognition and funding access.

Core Directions

Focus Areas of the Youth Development Plan

01

Active Citizenship

Mobilizing youth in civic exercises, barangay cleanups, volunteer networks, and participatory municipal budgeting.

02

Leadership & Governance

Rigorous capacity building for SK officials and councilors on ethical leadership, project management, and public transparency.

03

Education & Employment

Bridging livelihood initiatives, digital literacy, and vocational career orientations with corporate and municipal partners.

04

Health & Well-being

Promoting holistic wellness, mental health initiatives, sports development clinics, and drug abuse prevention awareness.

Flagship Initiatives

Programs Shaping San Mateo

Multi-Part Program

Civil Society Organization (CSO) Youth Academy

An intensive cohort training series designed to equip youth organization heads with organizational leadership, grant drafting, and project execution frameworks.

Annual Summit

Good Governance Summit: Kabataang San Mateo

A municipality-wide assembly that brings together student council officers and community leaders to dialogue directly with municipal officials.

Capacity Building

Project Ready to Serve: SK Development

Specialized institutional orientations covering ethical standards, fiscal responsibility, procurement laws, and municipal compliance reporting.
